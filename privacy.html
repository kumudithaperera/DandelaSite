Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what Dandela (“Dandela”, “we”) collects, why we collect it, and how it is protected. Dandela is a personal AI knowledge graph: an external memory for your notes, articles, and the connections between them. Your knowledge stays yours.

What we collect

  • Sources you capture. The text notes and URLs you add, along with the text we extract from them.
  • Derived knowledge. The chunks, embeddings, entities, relationships, and tags our pipeline creates from your sources so they can be searched and connected.
  • Account data. A sign-in identity (email) so only you can access your knowledge graph.

Why we collect it

Everything we store exists to build your knowledge graph and answer your questions grounded in your sources. We do not sell data, show ads, or build profiles.

How AI processing works

To understand and connect your sources, we send the captured text to the AI provider you configure: OpenAI or Gemini for embeddings, and OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic for chat and extraction. Your content is processed under each provider's terms, and you choose which provider handles your data.

Storage

Your sources and derived knowledge are stored in Supabase Postgres with row-level security, scoping every record to your account. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Transient processing data is not retained.

Third parties

Dandela relies on Supabase (database, auth, storage), Vercel (hosting), and the AI provider(s) you select. Each processes data under its own agreements.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete sources individually at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us through the app's Settings page and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can delete any source at any time. Where applicable law grants you rights over your personal data (access, correction, erasure, portability), you may exercise them by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
