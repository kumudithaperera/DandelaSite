Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what Dandela · Publish Now (“Dandela”, “we”) collects, why we collect it, and how it is protected. Dandela is a single-user app: only you use it, and your data stays yours.

What we collect

  • Account credentials. To publish on your behalf, you connect your Facebook Page, Instagram Business, and TikTok accounts. The access tokens for those accounts are stored and encrypted at rest.
  • Media you stage. The photo or video you pick for a post is uploaded to a private storage bucket so we can publish it.
  • Publish logs. Each publish attempt records a log row (platform, timestamp, result) so you can see what went out and when.
  • Basic auth data. A sign-in identity (email) so only you can access the app.

Why we collect it

Everything we store exists to do one thing: publish your media to the platforms you chose. We do not sell data, show ads, or build profiles.

How media is handled

Files you stage are uploaded directly to a private Supabase Storage bucket using signed URLs; they are not kept on the app server. The staged object is always deleted after the publish attempt completes, whether it succeeded or failed. Your account tokens are only readable through scoped security definer database functions and are never sent to your browser.

Encryption

Account tokens are encrypted at rest with pgsodium (AEAD). Connections to the app and to the platforms' APIs are encrypted in transit using TLS.

Third parties

Dandela shares only what is required to publish a post: your media and the caption are sent to the platform you selected (Meta and/or TikTok). Infrastructure is provided by Supabase (database, storage, auth) and Vercel (hosting), which process data under their own agreements.

Retention and deletion

Staging media is deleted after every publish attempt. Publish logs and account connections are kept so the app can function; you can disconnect accounts at any time from Settings. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can disconnect any connected platform at any time. Where applicable law grants you rights over your personal data (access, correction, erasure, portability), you may exercise them by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the app's Settings page.
