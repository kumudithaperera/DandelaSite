How it works

Three steps from thought to knowledge.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Jot a text note or paste a URL. Dandela fetches the page, strips it to readable text, and hashes the content so the same source is never stored twice.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Text is split at paragraph boundaries into ~1,200-character chunks with 200 characters of overlap, embedded, then mined for the entities and relationships that tie your knowledge together.

  3. 03

    Ask

    Search or ask anything. Hybrid retrieval finds the passages, the graph pulls in related sources, and the answer comes back with citations you can trace.

capture →extract text →chunk →embed →extract entities →link relationships →ask

Every source moves through the same pipeline, with a status you can watch: pending → processing → ready.

Knowledge model

Not a folder of notes. A graph.

Most tools store what you saved. Dandela stores what it means: the people, projects, decisions and ideas inside your sources, and the edges between them.

Sources & chunks

Every capture is kept whole and versioned, then split into embedded chunks so retrieval can land on the exact passage that answers you.

Entities

Recurring people, projects, companies and concepts are pulled out and given one canonical name, so ten mentions become one node.

person project company topic product technology place event concept

Relationships

Edges are only written when the text supports them, each carrying a confidence score and a link back to the passage it came from.

USES WORKS_ON PART_OF BUILT_WITH LEADS_TO HAS_DECISION RELATED_TO

Decisions & events

Choices you made, and when you made them, are recorded against the source that backs them, so you can ask why something is the way it is.

Projects

Group everything orbiting a piece of work: the notes, the articles, the decisions, the people involved.

Mentions

Each entity remembers every place it appeared, so you can walk from an idea back to every source that touched it.

Under the hood

Retrieval that reads like you remember.

Keyword search misses the passage that phrased it differently. Pure vector search misses the exact term. Dandela runs both and merges them, then lets the graph widen the net.

Hybrid search

PostgreSQL full-text ranking and cosine similarity run over the same chunks, merged and de-duplicated into one ranked result set.

Graph expansion

Once the best passages are found, Dandela pulls in other sources that share entities with them: context you would not have thought to search for.

Grounded answers

Answers are written only from retrieved evidence, and each citation is resolved back to the chunk and source it came from.

Provider-agnostic

OpenAI, Gemini or Anthropic for reasoning; OpenAI or Gemini for embeddings. Every vector is normalised to a single dimension, so you can switch without a rebuild.

How your sources are split
~1,200 characters Roughly two paragraphs per passage, short enough to quote exactly and long enough to still make sense on its own.
Why nothing falls through the cracks
200 characters shared Each passage overlaps the one before it, so a thought that straddles the boundary is never cut in half.
How meaning is stored
1,536 numbers per passage Every passage gets a numeric fingerprint of what it is about, so a search for “burnout” still finds “exhausted at work”.
How matches are ranked
By closest meaning Fingerprints pointing in the same direction rank highest, so the best passage wins even when it shares no keywords.

Privacy by design

Your knowledge stays yours.

Dandela is personal-first. Every table in the knowledge graph is scoped to your account by row-level security in the database itself, not by a check in application code that someone can forget to write.

  • Row-level security on sources, chunks, entities and edges
  • Every answer cites the source it came from
  • No training on your data, no shared index
  • AI providers you choose: OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic
