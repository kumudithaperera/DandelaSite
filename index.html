How it works

Three steps from thought to knowledge.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Jot a text note or paste a URL. PDFs and images are next.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Dandela extracts the text, chunks and embeds it, then maps the entities and relationships that tie your knowledge together.

  3. 03

    Ask

    Search or ask anything. Answers come back grounded in your own sources, with citations you can trace.

Privacy by design

Your knowledge stays yours.

Dandela is personal-first. Your sources live in your own space behind row-level security, and every answer is grounded in sources you can trace.

  • Your data, scoped to your account (RLS)
  • Every answer cites the source it came from
  • AI providers you choose: OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic
